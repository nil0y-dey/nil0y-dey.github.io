About

I'm an Industrial and Production Engineering graduate from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, and I'm looking for a PhD position at the intersection of machine learning, uncertainty quantification and operations.

My first-author paper in Expert Systems with Applications pairs Gaussian process regression with conformal calibration to give distribution-free prediction intervals for CO2 laser engraving — and turns those intervals into an automated accept / flag / reject quality gate. I'm now asking a harder question: when an ML surrogate is trained on calm operating data, can you still trust the decisions it drives once the supply chain is disrupted?

Before research full-time, I spent a year as a production engineer on electronics assembly lines at PRAN-RFL Group — which is where I learned that a model is only as good as the decision it supports.

Research

In progress Oct 2026 — present

Decision-regret validation of ML surrogates under supply-chain disruption

Independent research

Do ML surrogates trained on undisrupted operating data stay reliable for downstream inventory-policy decisions when disruptions hit? Rather than scoring prediction error, I evaluate surrogates by decision regret — the cost of acting on the surrogate instead of the truth.

  • A SimPy multi-echelon supply-chain simulator serves as ground truth, with independent replication on the ISOMORPH model.
  • Measuring policy-ranking stability, conformal coverage and sensitivity to disruption severity.

Published · ESWA Nov 2025 — Sep 2026

Uncertainty-aware ML and quality gating for laser engraving

Advisor: Dr. Mohammad Muhshin Aziz Khan · SUST

  • Benchmarked five architectures (GPR, SVR, MLP, random forest, polynomial ridge) with Grey Wolf Optimizer–tuned hyperparameters over a 125-run full-factorial design.
  • Conformalized GPR prediction intervals reach ≥ 94% empirical coverage at the 95% nominal level.
  • Built an uncertainty-aware gate that labels candidate process parameters accept, flag or reject.
  • Documented a subtle pitfall: variance-inflation checks miss exact algebraic rank deficiency in composite features.
Published · Heliyon Jan 2023 — May 2024

Undergraduate thesis: predicting laser-engraved groove geometry

Advisor: Dr. Mohammad Muhshin Aziz Khan · SUST

  • A four-hidden-layer MLP jointly predicts V-groove depth and width on PMMA from laser power, scanning speed and defocus length (five levels each).
  • R² > 0.99 on training and test sets; evaluated with MAPE, RMSE and MAE.
